# NetraCare Clinic Information

## Opening hours
NetraCare is open Monday to Saturday. Morning session: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Afternoon session: 2:30 PM to 6:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Appointments are 30 minutes long.

## Location and contact
NetraCare Eye Clinic is a fictional clinic located on 4th Cross, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038. The front desk phone number is +91 80 4000 1234 and the email address is hello@netracare.example. The front desk answers calls during opening hours.

## Parking and access
Free parking for about 20 cars is available in the basement. The clinic is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible, with a ramp at the main entrance and an accessible washroom. Please tell the front desk in advance if you need assistance.

## Languages spoken
Our doctors and front desk staff speak English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Marathi and Tulu. Ask the front desk to match you with a doctor who speaks your language.

## What to bring to your visit
Please bring a photo ID, any previous eye prescriptions or reports, your current glasses or contact lenses, a list of medicines you take, and your insurance card if you plan to use insurance. Arrive 10 minutes early for check-in.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, UPI, debit and credit cards. Payment is collected at the front desk after your consultation or procedure.

## Emergencies
NetraCare is not an emergency department. For sudden loss of vision, a serious eye injury or a chemical splash in the eye, go to the nearest emergency department or call local emergency services immediately. The assistant cannot give medical advice.
