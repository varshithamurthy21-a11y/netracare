# NetraCare Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need a referral?
No referral is needed to book a first visit with any NetraCare doctor.

## Can I bring a family member?
Yes. One family member can accompany you. If you will have dilating drops, we recommend that someone comes with you.

## How long will my visit take?
A typical visit is 30 to 45 minutes. If dilating drops or extra tests are needed, it can take longer.

## Can I drive after my visit?
If your eyes were dilated, vision can stay blurred for some hours. Ask the front desk or your doctor whether it is safe in your case; the assistant cannot advise on this.

## Do you offer online consultations?
Not currently. NetraCare provides in-person visits only.

## How do I get my reports?
Reports from partner lab tests are shared with your doctor. Ask the front desk how to collect a copy of your reports and visit summary.

## How do I change my phone number or email?
Open My profile in your patient account and update your details, or ask the front desk.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
Please contact the front desk, who can help you regain access to your account.

## Can I book for my child or parent?
Yes, the front desk can book an appointment for a family member. Tell them the patient's name and phone number.
