# NetraCare Doctors Overview

## How to choose a doctor
NetraCare has five specialists. Cataract and Lens Surgery covers cloudy or blurred vision and lens implants. Retina and Vitreous covers the back of the eye, diabetic eye screening, floaters and flashes. Glaucoma covers eye pressure checks and optic nerve monitoring. Cornea and Refractive covers general eye exams, glasses, contact lenses, dry eyes and LASIK consultations. Pediatric Ophthalmology and Squint covers children's eye care. If you are unsure, the Cornea and Refractive doctor is the usual starting point for a general check-up, and the front desk can re-route you.

## Children
Dr. Sneha Kulkarni sees children for eye exams, squint and lazy eye assessments. Parents can book a visit for their child through the patient account.

## Seeing a second doctor
Your doctor may suggest a visit with another NetraCare specialist. The front desk can book that visit for you, and your visit summary is available to the new doctor.
