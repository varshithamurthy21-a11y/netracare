# NetraCare Fees and Insurance

## Consultation fees
The standard consultation fee is 600 rupees for a first visit with any NetraCare doctor and 400 rupees for a follow-up visit within 30 days. A cataract or LASIK consultation with the surgeon is 800 rupees. These fees are indicative for this fictional clinic.

## Test prices
Indicative prices for tests done through our partner lab ClearView Diagnostics: OCT retina scan 1500 rupees, visual field test 1200 rupees, fundus photography 900 rupees, corneal topography 1400 rupees, tonometry 500 rupees, pachymetry 700 rupees. The partner lab confirms the final price when you book.

## Procedure price ranges
Indicative price ranges per eye: cataract surgery with a standard monofocal lens 25000 to 35000 rupees, cataract surgery with a premium lens 55000 to 85000 rupees, LASIK 40000 to 60000 rupees. The final cost depends on the lens or procedure type and is given after your consultation. NetraCare staff cannot quote a final price without the doctor's assessment.

## Insurance and cashless treatment
NetraCare accepts most major health insurance and third party administrator cards for cashless treatment on admitted procedures such as cataract surgery. Pre-authorisation is arranged by the front desk and usually takes 2 to 3 working days. Routine consultations and tests are usually paid at the front desk and claimed back by the patient.

## Payment and receipts
Payment is collected after the visit. A receipt is provided for every payment, and itemised bills for insurance claims are available at the front desk.
