# NetraCare Booking Policies

## Booking an appointment
Patients can book up to 14 days ahead with any NetraCare doctor, for up to three upcoming appointments at a time. Each appointment is 30 minutes. You can book through your patient account, the assistant chat, or by calling the front desk.

## Cancelling and rescheduling
You can cancel or reschedule your own booked appointments in your patient account or through the assistant chat, free of charge. Please cancel at least 2 hours before your appointment so the slot can be offered to another patient. Cancelling releases the slot immediately.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the front desk may ask you to reschedule so that other patients are not delayed. Please call us if you are running late.

## Missed appointments
Appointments that are not attended are recorded as no-shows. Repeated no-shows may mean the front desk asks you to call to book in future.

## Check-in and waiting time
Check in at the front desk when you arrive. The live queue shows your place in line and an estimated waiting time of roughly 12 minutes per patient ahead of you. Waiting times are estimates and can change.

## Urgent requests
If your booking message mentions words such as sudden vision loss, flashes, an eye injury or a chemical splash, our system flags it for the front desk team, who will review it and call you. This does not replace emergency care.

## Visit summaries
After your visit, your doctor may share a plain-language visit summary in your patient account. Summaries are written from the doctor's notes and are approved by the doctor before you can see them.

## Privacy
Your records are visible only to you and to NetraCare staff who need them for your care. The assistant never shares one patient's information with another.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant can answer questions about NetraCare, suggest which specialist's calendar to look at, and help you book, cancel or reschedule appointments with your approval. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment guidance.
