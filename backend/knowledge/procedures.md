# NetraCare Procedures and Tests

## Comprehensive eye examination
A comprehensive eye examination checks visual acuity, eye movement, eye pressure and the front and back of the eye. Dilating drops are sometimes used, which can blur vision and increase light sensitivity for a few hours, so please arrange someone to accompany you. The visit takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

## Cataract surgery consultation
A cataract consultation is a discussion with the cataract surgeon about your vision, the measurements taken for the lens implant, the available lens options and the surgery schedule. It does not involve surgery on the same day. Dr. Ananya Rao leads cataract and lens surgery at NetraCare.

## LASIK and refractive consultation
A refractive consultation includes measurements of your eyes, a discussion of vision correction options and what the evaluation involves. Contact lens wearers are usually asked to stop wearing lenses for a period before measurements; the front desk will tell you the exact duration when you book. Dr. Rohan Shetty handles refractive consultations.

## Glaucoma screening
Glaucoma screening usually includes an eye pressure check (tonometry), an optic nerve examination and sometimes an OCT scan and a visual field test. Dr. Meera Iyer follows glaucoma patients over the long term.

## Diabetic eye screening
Diabetic eye screening examines the retina and often includes retinal photography or an OCT scan. Dr. Karthik Menon performs diabetic eye screening. Bring your recent blood sugar reports.

## Paediatric eye assessment
A children's eye assessment is play-based where possible and checks vision, eye alignment and eye movement. Dr. Sneha Kulkarni sees children. Bring your child's favourite toy or book to keep them comfortable.

## Contact lens fitting
A contact lens fitting includes measurements, trial lenses and instructions on handling the lenses. It takes about 45 minutes.

## OCT scan, visual field test and other diagnostic tests
NetraCare works with our partner lab ClearView Diagnostics for tests such as OCT retina scans, visual field tests, fundus photography, corneal topography, tonometry and pachymetry. Your doctor may request a test, or you can ask the front desk about booking one.
