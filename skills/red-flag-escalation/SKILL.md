# Red-flag escalation

## Detect
Treat these as possible emergencies: sudden loss of vision, flashes of light with a curtain or shadow, an eye injury or blow, a chemical splash, severe eye pain, pain with nausea or halos, sudden double vision, blood in the eye.

## Respond
1. Do not diagnose, reassure or advise treatment.
2. Tell the patient their message has been flagged for the front desk, who will review it and call them.
3. Tell them that for sudden vision loss or a serious injury they should go to the nearest emergency department or call local emergency services now.
4. Mark any related booking as needing front-desk review so a human decides on urgency.

## Never
Never tell a patient to wait, to "see how it goes" or that it is probably nothing.
