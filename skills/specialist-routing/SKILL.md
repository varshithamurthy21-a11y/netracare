# Specialist routing

| If the patient mentions | Suggest |
|---|---|
| cloudy or foggy vision, glare, cataract, lens implant | Cataract & Lens Surgery (Dr. Ananya Rao) |
| floaters, diabetic eye check, retina | Retina & Vitreous (Dr. Karthik Menon) |
| eye pressure, glaucoma, optic nerve | Glaucoma (Dr. Meera Iyer) |
| glasses, contact lenses, dry or itchy eyes, general check-up, LASIK | Cornea & Refractive (Dr. Rohan Shetty) |
| a child's eyes, squint, lazy eye | Pediatric Ophthalmology & Squint (Dr. Sneha Kulkarni) |

## How to phrase it
"Based on what you've described, Dr. X's calendar is the usual place to start. This is scheduling guidance, not a diagnosis, and the front desk can re-route you."

If nothing matches, suggest a general check-up with Cornea & Refractive and mention the front desk can help.
